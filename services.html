Payroll Services

Payroll is a core service, offered in two packages.

Package 1: Payroll Processing

For employers who need accurate, on-time payroll.

  • Monthly or agreed-frequency payroll calculations
  • Salary and wage calculations
  • Overtime, bonuses and allowances where applicable
  • PAYE and UIF calculations, and SDL where applicable
  • Payslip preparation and distribution
  • Payroll reports and payroll journals
  • Employee payroll record administration
  • Leave and payroll-related record administration where included

Package 2: Payroll and Compliance Support

Everything in Package 1 focus areas, plus compliance support.

  • Payroll processing and payslips
  • Payroll-related reconciliations
  • EMP201 preparation and submission support
  • EMP501 reconciliation assistance
  • IRP5/IT3(a) employee tax certificate administration
  • Payroll-related SARS query support and supporting documentation
  • Payroll expense and liability reconciliations with accounting records

How the packages differ: Package 1 produces the payroll and payslips. Package 2 also reconciles payroll to your accounting records and supports your SARS payroll submissions and queries. Payroll is available subject to an initial assessment: your needs, software, appropriate access and authorisation, and applicable legal or registration requirements. Quotes are tailored; no prices are published.
