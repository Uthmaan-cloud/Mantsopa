About Mantsopa Financial Services

Careful accounting support, built on audit experience.

Background

Mantsopa Financial Services was started to give small businesses organised, dependable accounting support. The owner holds a BCom in Accounting and a BCom Honours in Accounting.

The owner has practical external audit experience, including financial reporting, audit documentation, substantive testing, analytical review, reconciliations and audit supervision. That training shapes how we work: records are checked, reconciled and documented so they stand up to scrutiny.

How we work

  • Attention to detail: we reconcile and review before we report.
  • Confidentiality: financial information is handled responsibly and only for the purpose of your engagement.
  • Professional communication: clear updates and plain-language explanations.
  • Organised records: tidy, well-supported accounting files.
  • Practical support: advice sized for small and owner-managed businesses.

We are transparent about scope: we will tell you if a service needs a registration or authorisation we do not hold, and we do not offer audit or assurance services.
