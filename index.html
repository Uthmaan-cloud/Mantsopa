Common questions

What does bookkeeping include?

Recording and organising transactions, reconciling bank, customer and supplier accounts, and producing monthly management accounts. We can also clean up overdue records.

What is the difference between the two payroll packages?

Payroll Processing covers calculations, payslips and payroll reports. Payroll and Compliance Support adds reconciliations, EMP201 and EMP501 support, IRP5/IT3(a) certificate administration and SARS query support.

Can you start payroll for me immediately?

Payroll is offered after an initial assessment of your needs, software, access and authorisation, and the applicable requirements.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different, so we provide a tailored quote after understanding your needs. Prices are not listed on this website.

Do you perform audits?

No. We prepare accounting records and schedules that can be handed to your external auditor. We do not offer statutory audits, independent reviews or other assurance engagements.

How do I get a quote?

Complete the quote request form, email us, or use WhatsApp where available. Please do not send employee ID numbers, bank details or individual salaries in your first enquiry.
