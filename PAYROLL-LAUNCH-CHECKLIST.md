# Internal checklist: before accepting the first payroll client
(Not for publishing on the website.)

## Knowledge
- [ ] PAYE tables and calculation, UIF, SDL thresholds and rules
- [ ] BCEA basics: leave, overtime, notice, deductions, payslip content
- [ ] EMP201 monthly cycle and EMP501 bi-annual reconciliation
- [ ] IRP5/IT3(a) certificate rules and tax year-end timing
- [ ] COIDA/Compensation Fund returns, where relevant
- [ ] Treatment of benefits, fringe benefits and retirement funding

## Software
- [ ] Choose payroll software, check its current free tier or cost against your R0 budget
- [ ] Practise on dummy data and reconcile a full month to a trial balance
- [ ] Decide how backups and secure document sharing will work

## Access and authorisation
- [ ] Written client authority to act on payroll and SARS matters
- [ ] SARS eFiling access arrangement per client, never share passwords informally
- [ ] Engagement letter defining scope, deadlines, fees and responsibilities

## Compliance and risk
- [ ] Confirm professional registration questions (see README)
- [ ] POPIA safeguards for employee data (access control, encryption, retention, deletion)
- [ ] Professional indemnity insurance
- [ ] Review on a dummy client by someone experienced in payroll

## Website
- [ ] Do not describe payroll clients, certifications or software expertise until true
